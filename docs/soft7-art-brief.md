# Soft7 art brief

This is the handoff for the cards. One source of truth. The pieces in **Still to hand over** were not in the earlier pass. They are part of the brief now.

Proofs come back for approval before anything is minted.

## Still to hand over

**Eyes stay fixed.** The eyes are the identity. That is non-negotiable. They lock on the viewer and they do not move with the potion, the chakra, or the bloom. Color and mask can change around them. The eyes do not.

**The amulet is the quietest layer.** It shifts with the chakra color, subtle enough that you only notice it when you are looking for it. It is what ties the card together. In the bloom it is also first: the amulet catches the color before anything else.

**Clothing and the belt are in the card.** Both are silent details, not loud. They read when you look, and they stay out of the way when you do not. The belt buckle is the last stage of the bloom, and it stays quiet.

**Media.** PNG or GIF. One image per token. The JPEGs in `images/033.jpg`–`images/039.jpg` are test versions. They are not the art to ship.

**Go-live.** The original clock was 10:00. That time has drifted. The target is still tonight, 27 September 2026 Pacific.

## Paint

Paintbrush, not flat digital. Every character comes off the mascot. Face paint on all of them, no exceptions, and no two characters share the same face paint.

Seven tiers, seven mask designs, from the base mask up to the most complex.

The potion is the engine. The card blooms slowly, in this order:

1. Amulet catches the chakra color.
2. Mask paint blooms.
3. Belt buckle.

The eyes stay out of that sequence.

Colors differ across tiers and stay tied to the potion and the chakra system.

## Who is in the frame

Tigers are thin and lean. Jedi or wizard, heroic pose, stark side, no extra colors.

Foxes keep the pristine mascot look. Colorful side, full body visible, nothing cropped.

Every character is centered, eyes locked on the viewer, feet on solid ground, in a dynamic Star Wars stance.
