The seven

Staking

Holding a Soft7 card is the stake. There is no lockup contract and no advertised rate. Tokens 1–7 are the only position that earns.

Once a quarter the vault pays ETH to those holders, pro-rata, one share per card. A wallet with two cards receives two shares.

Vault 0x6B0E22d322c967DFBDB57D027cE53D1D32F7711A

Dividend

Every secondary sale pays a 7.5% creator fee. 1.5% of the sale price — one fifth of that fee — is the holder dividend.

Soft7RoyaltyRouter sends 80% to the creator and 20% to the vault. The vault pays that dividend on-chain, quarterly, to holders of tokens 1–7.
