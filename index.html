The seven

Staking

Every Soft7 card is staked inside this project. No outside protocol holds it. The card earns from the treasury slice, and a longer hold takes a larger share.

Sell it and the mask cracks. Unpaid rewards go back to the treasury, the hold resets, and the card earns less until a full quarter of holding heals the mask.

Dividend

Every secondary sale pays a 7.5% creator fee. 1.5% of the sale price — the treasury slice of that fee — is the holder dividend.

Soft7RoyaltyRouter sends 80% of the fee to the creator and the rest to the vault. Quarterly, the vault pays that dividend on-chain to holders of tokens 1–7.
